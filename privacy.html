Privacy Policy

Application: Slipe AI • Entity: Paceaitechnologies (OPC) Private Limited • Last Updated: October 2, 2026

We respect your privacy. Slipe AI is designed with privacy in mind, and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal photos, videos, or information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

1. Information We Collect

Slipe AI does not intentionally collect or maintain personal information about you for advertising, profiling, or data-selling purposes. We do not require you to provide personal information simply to use the core image creation and photo enhancement features of Slipe AI.

Slipe AI does not collect or sell:

  • Your personal photos
  • Your videos
  • Your AI-generated images
  • Your prompts or creative ideas
  • Your screen content
  • Your contacts
  • Your location

2. AI Image, Photo, and Video Processing

Slipe AI allows you to generate AI images and enhance existing photos and videos.

To provide these features, media content that you choose to submit for enhancement or generation is temporarily transmitted to the neural AI processing infrastructure required to fulfill your request.

Slipe AI does not use your submitted photos or videos for advertising, sell them, or use them to build advertising profiles. Where technically possible, submitted content is processed only for the purpose of fulfilling your requested enhancement or generation.

3. Photos, Videos, and Storage Permissions

If you choose to use Slipe AI’s photo enhancement, upscaling, colorization, or video processing features, you select media files from your device.

Slipe AI accesses this media content only when necessary to execute the enhancement feature you explicitly choose. We do not sell or use your media for advertising.

4. Third-Party Neural AI Services

Some Slipe AI creation features rely on secure cloud AI services to render images, upscale resolution, or process videos. When you request a feature, the media necessary to fulfill your request is sent over encrypted connections.

Slipe AI does not sell this media or use it for advertising purposes.

5. Analytics and Security

Slipe AI does not intentionally use your personal media for advertising tracking or behavioral profiling. We take reasonable technical measures to protect all media involved in providing Slipe AI.

6. Account and Data Deletion Policy

At PaceAI, we give our users full control over their personal data. In compliance with Google Play Store policies, users have two convenient ways to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal information:

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended)

You can permanently delete your account directly from within the PaceAI mobile app:

  1. Open the PaceAI app on your device.
  2. Tap on the side menu and open Settings (or open your Profile screen).
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm by tapping DELETE FOREVER.

Your account, profile information, chat history, and generated media will be permanently purged from our servers immediately.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email (Without App Installed)

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion via email:

  1. Send an email from your registered email address to paceai.support@gmail.com
  2. Set subject line to: Account Deletion Request - PaceAI
  3. Include your registered email, full name, and the statement: "I request the permanent deletion of my PaceAI account and all associated personal data."

Processing Timeframe & Data Retention

In-app deletion is processed immediately. Email requests are verified within 48–72 hours and completed within 30 days. All profile information, conversation history, generated media, and diagnostic logs are permanently purged. Financial/invoice records (processed via Razorpay) are retained for the minimum statutory period required by Indian tax laws.

For full policy details, visit our dedicated Account Deletion Page.
